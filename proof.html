Discharge — claim your DOT Resolve today's field

How claims work: every sealed unit discharges once, from 6 hours after its day seals, for a 0.0002 BNB fee. The amount is fixed by the tier of the day it sealed — SPARK 2,500 DOT · CHARGED 12,500 DOT · OVERDRIVE 60,000 DOT · SURGE 250,000 DOT. Claims never expire. No price is promised for DOT — the only promise is what the contract does, and the contract is public: read it, check it, then decide.
