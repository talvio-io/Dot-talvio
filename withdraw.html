Connect the owner wallet to withdraw. Any other wallet is blocked by the contract itself (onlyOwner).
Exactly what withdraw() does (TalvioGenerate.sol, owner functions): it sends the entire contract balance to owner() in one call and emits Withdrawn(owner, amount). There is no amount and no destination to choose — no partial withdraw, no withdraw to another address. If the balance is 0 the call reverts with "nothing to withdraw". The BNB comes from paid resolves (0.00026 / 0.001 BNB), discharge fees (0.0002 BNB) and DPASS sales (0.0012 BNB). The 600,000,000 DOT claim pool is a token balance, not BNB — withdraw() never touches it.

How to withdraw

  1. Connect the owner wallet (…) on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56). The button stays disabled for any other wallet.
  2. Press WITHDRAW ALL BNB and confirm in your wallet. The page first simulates the call — if the contract would revert, nothing is sent.
  3. Wait for the receipt. The transaction hash appears below with a BscScan link. The balance on this page refreshes to 0 after it confirms.
  4. No wallet here? Use BscScan directly instead: open the contract on BscScan, connect the owner wallet, go to Write Contract → withdraw and send it: bscscan.com/address/0xFfC521…dB2C#writeContract ↗
