Class — fixed per unit in its published metadata and seeded across the full #1–#10,000 range: Prime 3,000 · Pixel Dot 2,800 · Stealth Dot 2,700 · Aurum Dot 1,500 in total. #1 is the Genesis Dot, an Aurum-class one-of-one. Any lane can draw any class — the sequence, not the lane, decides.

Field Notes

Can I see the NFT artwork anywhere?+
No — and that is deliberate. The collection artwork is sealed: no unit image, class illustration or specimen render appears on this site or in its machine files before the reveal. What is public now is everything around the art — the four classes and their shares, the dot-density of each class, the Charge Day rules, the Mosaic and the DOT claim table. You resolve a sealed unit; what it looks like is the reveal's business, and the reveal date will be announced by the project, not leaked by this page.
How does the free mint work now — is it really daily?+
Yes. Holders of a Binance Agent ID resolve 1 unit free per wallet per day (0 BNB, network fee only), after answering the day's on-chain arithmetic question. The free quota is 150 units per day inside the 250-unit day cap, and it resets at every seal, 12:00 UTC. There is no lifetime free allowance anymore — yesterday's free says nothing about today's.
I have no Binance Agent ID — is there a way in?+
Yes — DPASS, the Dot Pass. One pass costs 0.0012 BNB (supply unlimited), and a wallet holding one resolves units and discharges them with no Agent ID and no anti-bot arithmetic question: at the free gate the pass stands in for the Agent ID, and the question is never asked. Everything else is identical — the same 1 free unit per day, the same paid ladder (0.00026 → 0.001 BNB, max 10/day), the same discharge fee and DOT claims. The pass changes who can enter, never what anything costs.
When do DPASS sales open — and when does the pass start working?+
Two different moments, stated plainly. Sales open together with Field Nº 1 — the day the owner opens mint and the first field starts. The pass lane activates at Field Nº 4: a pass bought during Fields Nº 1–3 is a key bought early, and it starts opening gates when Field Nº 4 opens, not before. The contracts are deployed, but mint is still closed — Field Nº 1 has not opened, so sales are not live yet and no pass can be bought today.
What does a full paid day cost?+
Paid counts your units today, including the free one. Units 2–5 cost 0.00026 BNB each and units 6–10 cost 0.001 BNB each, max 10 units per wallet per day. A wallet taking the free unit plus all 9 paid units pays 4 × 0.00026 + 5 × 0.001 = 0.00604 BNB in total. The paid lane is open in parallel from the day's first block — never gated behind the free lane.
What decides the day's Charge Tier?+
Unique wallets that day — not volume. Under 25 unique wallets seals as SPARK (Core Lv.1), 25–99 as CHARGED (Core Lv.2), 100+ as OVERDRIVE (Core Lv.3). The tier is written into every unit's metadata at the seal and never changes, which is why showing up — and bringing another wallet with you — raises the DOT claim of every unit sealed that day.
What is Discharge, and what does it pay?+
The second act. From seal +6 hours, each sealed unit can discharge once for a 0.0002 BNB fee and claim DOT: 2,500 DOT on a SPARK day, 12,500 DOT on a CHARGED day, 60,000 DOT on an OVERDRIVE day, and 250,000 DOT for a rare SURGE unit with a gold core (≈3% of units, seed % 1000 ≥ 970). Claims never expire. The DOT amount per unit is fixed by the tier — what is not promised is any price for DOT.
What is DOT — is it launched? Can I buy it?+
DOT is the project's own token: total supply 1,000,000,000 DOT, with 60% (600,000,000 DOT) held by the NFT contract as the funded discharge claim pool. It is deployed on BNB Chain at 0x779ee8B473c09d39BBeFA78636c56C85BfF4456a, and no price is promised. The project has announced one dated milestone: $DOT lists on Uniswap (BNB Chain) · 3 December 2026 — an announced date, nothing more; that listing has not happened yet, and there is no official market for DOT until it does.
When does resolving open?+
When the owner opens mint. The contracts are already deployed on BNB Chain — NFT 0xFfC521D504F20bd7bCd0871B9B39BA9501F5dB2C, DOT 0x779ee8B473c09d39BBeFA78636c56C85BfF4456a — both Sourcify-verified, and the 600,000,000 DOT claim pool is funded. But mintOpen is still false and Field Nº 1 has not started: resolving opens only on the owner's Open Mint signal. Until then the Charge Day panel shows the true closed state — zero counts, no tier, no running clock — and the page's only simulations are its labelled specimen tools: no wallet connects here and no funds can move from this page; on-chain mint wiring from the website is a later step.
Which lane can pull an Aurum Dot or a SURGE?+
Both. Class is rolled at resolve, not assigned by lane: Prime 3,000 · Pixel Dot 2,800 · Stealth Dot 2,700 · Aurum Dot 1,500 (token #1 is the unique Genesis Dot). SURGE is a separate seeded trait (~3%) that can land on a free or a paid unit alike.
