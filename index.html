Check a unit number

Enter any unit from #1 to #10,000. Its class is read from the unit's own published metadata — classes are seeded across the whole range, not stacked in blocks. Everything else is chain-first: if the unit has been resolved, its field, core level, SURGE status and exact DOT claim are read live from the contract. If it has not been resolved yet, the checker says so plainly — an unminted unit has no on-chain data, and none is invented for it.

How to read it

Class — fixed per unit in its published metadata and seeded across the full #1–#10,000 range: Prime 3,000 · Pixel Dot 2,800 · Stealth Dot 2,700 · Aurum Dot 1,500 in total. #1 is the Genesis Dot, an Aurum-class one-of-one. Any lane can draw any class — the sequence, not the lane, decides.

SURGE — if seed % 1000 ≥ 970 (≈3% of units), the unit carries a gold core trait (its artwork, like every unit's, is sealed until the reveal) and claims 250,000 DOT at Discharge, whatever its day's tier.

Core Level & DOT claim — both come from the Charge Tier of the day the unit sealed: SPARK → Lv.1 · 2,500 DOT, CHARGED → Lv.2 · 12,500 DOT, OVERDRIVE → Lv.3 · 60,000 DOT. Neither ever changes after the seal.

Can I see the NFT artwork anywhere?+
No — and that is deliberate. The collection artwork is sealed: no unit image, class illustration or specimen render appears on this site or in its machine files before the reveal. How does the free mint work now — is it really daily?+
Yes. Holders of a Binance Agent ID resolve 1 unit free per wallet per day (0 BNB, network fee only), after answering the day's on-chain arithmetic question. The free quota is 150 units per day inside the 250-unit day cap, and it resets at every seal, 12:00 UTC. There is no lifetime free allowance anymore — yesterday's free says nothing about today's.
I have no Binance Agent ID — is there a way in?+
Yes — DPASS, the Dot Pass. One pass costs 0.0012 BNB (supply unlimited), and a wallet holding one resolves units and discharges them with no Agent ID and no anti-bot arithmetic question: at the free gate the pass stands in for the Agent ID, and the question is never asked. Everything else is identical — the same 1 free unit per day, the same paid ladder (0.00026 → 0.001 BNB, max 10/day), the same discharge fee and DOT claims. The pass changes who can enter, never what anything costs.
When do DPASS sales open — and when does the pass start working?+
Two different moments, stated plainly. Sales opened together with Field Nº 1 — on 18 November 2026, the day the owner opened mint and the first field started. The pass lane activates at Field Nº 4: a pass bought during Fields Nº 1–3 is a key bought early, and it starts opening gates when Field Nº 4 opens, not before. Field Nº 1 opened on 18 November 2026, so sales are live and a pass can be bought today — from this page, via buyPass().
What does a full paid day cost?+
Paid counts your units today, including the free one. Units 2–5 cost 0.00026 BNB each and units 6–10 cost 0.001 BNB each, max 10 units per wallet per day. A wallet taking the free unit plus all 9 paid units pays 4 × 0.00026 + 5 × 0.001 = 0.00604 BNB in total. The paid lane is open in parallel from the day's first block — never gated behind the free lane.
What decides the day's Charge Tier?+
Unique wallets that day — not volume. Under 25 unique wallets seals as SPARK (Core Lv.1), 25–99 as CHARGED (Core Lv.2), 100+ as OVERDRIVE (Core Lv.3). The tier is written into every unit's metadata at the seal and never changes, which is why showing up — and bringing another wallet with you — raises the DOT claim of every unit sealed that day.
What is Discharge, and what does it pay?+
The second act. From seal +6 hours, each sealed unit can discharge once for a 0.0002 BNB fee and claim DOT: 2,500 DOT on a SPARK day, 12,500 DOT on a CHARGED day, 60,000 DOT on an OVERDRIVE day, and 250,000 DOT for a rare SURGE unit with a gold core (≈3% of units, seed % 1000 ≥ 970). Claims never expire. The DOT amount per unit is fixed by the tier — what is not promised is any price for DOT.
