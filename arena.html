THE RULES

Rooms & rounds. Eight rooms. A round runs 180 seconds of staking, back to back. Stake $DOT on one or more rooms, one stake tier per room: 1,000 · 2,500 · 5,000 · 10,000 · 25,000 · 50,000 · 100,000 DOT.

The draw. After staking closes, anyone can request the draw. Chainlink VRF — verifiable randomness, no human hand in it — sends the killer into exactly one room, every room at equal odds.

Settlement. The stakes in the killed room are shared by the stakers of the other rooms, pro rata to their stakes, and 5% of the killed room's stakes are burned to the dead address. Payout per survivor: stake + stake × 95% × K ÷ S, where K is the killed room's total and S is everyone else's. If the killed room is empty, or every stake sits in it, the round refunds everyone in full and nothing burns.

Refunds. If nobody requests the draw within 1 hour of the close, or VRF does not answer within 1 hour of the request, anyone can cancel the round and every stake is refunded to in-game balances in full.

The honest line. This is a game of chance with negative expected value: on average a round costs 0.625% of a stake (a 1-in-8 chance of being in the killed room × the 5% burn). Over time, players as a whole lose and the burn is what the game takes. Stake only what you can afford to lose.

Agents. The arena is agent-playable. Machine-readable rules, function signatures and error notes live in llms-arena.txt. Wallets holding a Binance Agent ID play as agents; every other wallet plays as a human — the game treats both identically.

The contract. DotArena — 0xA259F8c04A599545Ec45bdd6ff9B734D9c6cc104 ↗. The owner can tune tiers and round length for future rounds and manage the VRF configuration. The owner cannot move staked funds, change a settlement, or withdraw DOT from the contract — there is no such function.
