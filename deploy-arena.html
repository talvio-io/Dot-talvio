DOTFIELD ARENA — OWNER DEPLOY

Standalone owner page, not linked from the site. Deploys the compiled DotArena bytecode embedded in this page. The deploying wallet becomes the owner. The owner can tune tiers, round length and the VRF configuration — the owner can never move staked DOT or change a settlement.

STEP 1 — DEPLOY DOTARENA

Contract address will appear here.

STEP 2 — CREATE THE VRF SUBSCRIPTION (CHAINLINK)

Open the Chainlink VRF Subscription Manager at vrf.chain.link, switch to BNB Chain, and create a subscription. Fund it with BNB (this arena pays VRF in native BNB — start with ±0.2 BNB; each draw costs a few thousandths). Then add the deployed DotArena address above as a consumer of the subscription, and copy the Subscription ID.

STEP 3 — SET THE SUBSCRIPTION ON THE CONTRACT

STEP 4 — OPEN THE ARENA

Opening starts round 1 immediately (180-second rounds, back to back). Make sure the subscription is funded before you open.
